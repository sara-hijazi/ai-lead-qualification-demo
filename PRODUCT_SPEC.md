# AI Lead Qualification — Interactive Portfolio Demo

## Purpose and boundaries
Demonstrate the behavior and business value of the existing AI Lead Qualification System through Incoming Lead → Analysis → Qualification Decision → Reasoning → Recommended Action → Company-side result. This public demo reproduces behavior locally; it is not the original production workflow. All execution is in the browser, without paid APIs, backend, database, authentication, analytics, or automation runtime. React, Vite, strict TypeScript, minimal dependencies, static hosting.

## Languages
Centralized English and Arabic translations, shared components, AR | EN selector. Arabic is the default on first load, with RTL document and interface direction. English remains available with LTR through the selector. Arabic copy is gender neutral, without diacritics or gender-specific direct address.

## Appearance and global navigation
Dark is the default appearance, with an optional professional Light mode using the same blue/green brand and semantic red statuses. The shared header has minimal sun/moon and Home icons on every screen. Theme persists across navigation and is saved locally when storage is available; Arabic remains the initial language regardless of theme. Home returns directly to Welcome and clears the inquiry and processing progress while retaining language and theme. Home on Welcome is a no-op. Leaving Processing cancels its timers.

## Deterministic engine
Isolated reusable engine, no opaque score. Supported service means any service other than Other. Meaningful need means at least 20 trimmed characters and at least four whitespace-separated words. Viable budget means 5,000 SAR or more. Near-term intent means Immediately or Within 30 days. Unsupported service or materially absent business need → NOT QUALIFIED. Supported service and meaningful need, viable budget and near-term intent → QUALIFIED. Otherwise → NEEDS NURTURING. Qualified priority is High; nurturing priority is Medium with viable budget, otherwise Low; not qualified priority is Low. Summary, reasons, fit labels and action derive from inputs and decision. Reasons explain each condition; actions distinguish missing scope, unsupported service, budget readiness and timeline readiness.
